# 01 · Vision Document

## Mission
Make finding and listing property in India **trustworthy, fast, and honest** — starting where the big portals never bothered to go deep: tier-2/3 religious-economy cities and student housing.

## Vision
By year 5: the default property platform for **every campus city and pilgrimage city in North India**, and the most trusted listings brand in the markets we operate — measured not by traffic but by *lead quality dealers renew for* and *zero-fraud reputation renters trust*.

## USP (in priority order)
1. **Every listing verified before it goes live** — approval queue, geo-pin, real photos, live phone. The portals aggregate; we curate.
2. **Student housing as a first-class ecosystem** — per-bed pricing, university-distance search, college pages. Nobody serves this properly below metro tier.
3. **Hyperlocal depth over national breadth** — 100% of Mathura's real inventory beats 2% of India's.
4. **WhatsApp-native** — the actual operating system of Indian real estate.
5. **Speed** — sub-second pages on 4G; the portals ship 8MB of ads.

## Business positioning
> "The trusted property platform of Braj — and India's student housing platform."

- **Against 99acres/MagicBricks/Housing:** they are *listing dumps* monetizing dealer desperation; thin in tier-2/3; zero verification credibility. We position on trust + depth, not inventory count.
- **Against NoBroker:** they charge tenants and exclude brokers. In tier-2/3, brokers ARE the supply; we embrace and upgrade them. Finding is always free on GharBazaar.
- **Against Stanza/Zolo (student):** they are asset-heavy operators in metros only. We are the *marketplace* for the 95% of PGs/hostels they'll never operate.
- **Against the real competitor — WhatsApp groups and the corner broker:** we don't fight them, we absorb them: free listings, WhatsApp-first leads, assisted onboarding, and a verification badge that makes a small broker look bigger than he is.

## Expansion strategy — concentric, university-led
```
Stage 0  Mathura + Vrindavan (Braj core)          → own it completely
Stage 1  + NCR student housing ONLY (beachhead)   → college pages, PG operators
Stage 2  Agra–Aligarh corridor (campus cities)    → repeatable playbook v1
Stage 3  UP tier-2 (Kanpur, Lucknow, Prayagraj)   → playbook v2 + services revenue
Stage 4  NCR general Buy/Rent (funded fight)       → only with war chest + brand
Stage 5  National student housing network          → every campus city in India
```
**The repeatable unit is the campus city, not the metro.** Each expansion = data seeding (city/areas/universities) + 2-person field team + ambassador program. Zero code.

## v1.1 — Founder Review additions

### Why now
1. **Enrollment wave:** NEP-driven higher-ed expansion keeps pushing students into campus cities; private universities (GLA, Sanskriti class) grow faster than their hostel capacity.
2. **The Jewar effect:** Noida International Airport + Yamuna Expressway corridor is repricing land from Greater Noida to Mathura — investor demand is arriving *now*, with no trusted local discovery layer.
3. **Rails are ready:** UPI + WhatsApp penetration means tier-3 sellers can be onboarded, verified, and paid digitally — impossible five years ago.
4. **Incumbent trust rot is peaking:** fake-listing fatigue is a common complaint; a verified brand can convert it.

### Market sizing (directional estimates — validate in-market, do not pitch as fact)
- **TAM:** India online property classifieds/adtech revenue ≈ ₹2,000–2,500 Cr/yr and growing; student living rent flow >₹1L Cr/yr across ~10M+ migrating students.
- **SAM:** discovery + monetization layer for campus cities + UP/NCR corridor.
- **SOM (Y1):** Braj listings market + NCR student clusters — target ₹25–35L ARR post-liquidity.

### Moat / copycat defense ("what if 99acres copies college pages?")
They can render pages; they cannot cheaply replicate: field-verified supply at tier-3 economics (their cost structure is metro-sales-led), per-bed inventory freshness (requires operator relationships), lead-verified review corpus, locality-grain demand data, and a local trust brand built season by season. Our defense is operational depth, not UI.

### Risk register
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| SEO/Google dependency (organic is our demand engine) | Retention loops (alerts, WhatsApp re-entry), brand-search growth KPI, direct traffic via offline brand |
| Incumbent response (free premium dump in Mathura) | Relationships + demand-data digest + founding-dealer lock-in; they can't sustain tier-3 field ops |
| Season concentration (student revenue peaks Apr–Jul) | Rentals/buy smooth the curve; season fallback = next intake (Jan) with retention list |
| Regulatory (RERA ad rules, IT Rules, DPDP) | Compliance items in 10§7; grievance officer; conservative badge claims |
| Key-person (founder-led sales) | Playbook documentation from day one; city-lead hiring gate in 12§9 |
| Verification over-claim liability | 10§8 honesty constraints; legal review of badge copy |

### Metric horizons (added to §Success metrics)
Launch gates: T+3 months. Liquidity: T+6. Student wedge: first full season. Monetization: T+9. Expansion-ready: T+12. **"Qualified lead" is defined precisely in 12§8 — the north star inherits that definition.**
