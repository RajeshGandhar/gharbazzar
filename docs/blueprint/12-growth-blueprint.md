# 12 · Growth Blueprint

## 0. The strategic spine
Marketplaces die from empty shelves, not bad code. Sequence: **supply → trust → demand → retention → revenue → expansion.** Every stage has a gate; we do not skip gates because a dashboard looks done.

## 1. Pre-launch (T-90 → T-0): supply first, by hand
- **Field onboarding:** 2-person team walks Mathura dealer clusters (Holi Gate, Krishna Nagar, station road) with assisted listing on a phone. Target: **50 dealers, 200+ listings live before any marketing rupee.** Founding-dealer offer: free Gold 6 months + founding badge (permanent).
- **PG blitz:** every PG/hostel within 3km of GLA + Sanskriti, onboarded via operator WhatsApp groups + campus-gate walks. Target: 80% coverage of the GLA corridor.
- **Owner seeding:** "List free, we do it for you" WhatsApp forwards through society groups.
- **Content pre-seed:** 15 locality guides + college housing guides written and indexed before launch (SEO needs crawl time).
- **Timing:** public launch **T-6 weeks before admission season peak** (season = April–July; work backward from the target year's counselling dates).

## 2. Launch (Mathura + Vrindavan): demand ignition
- **SEO** live day one (06) — the compounding channel; everything else buys time until it compounds.
- **Performance marketing (small, surgical):** Google Ads on high-intent local queries ("pg near gla university", "plot in vrindavan") — tier-3 CPCs are pocket change; kill anything above target CPL.
- **Social:** Instagram/YouTube Shorts — property tours, locality tours, "PG mein kya check karein" — local-language, founder-fronted is fine and free.
- **Campus:** ambassadors at GLA/Sanskriti (₹ per verified student signup + listing reports), admission-consultant partnerships, fresher WhatsApp/Telegram groups.
- **Offline (cheap, high-trust in tier-3):** auto-rickshaw backs near campuses, banners at admission-season choke points, dealer-shop "Listed on GharBazaar ✓ Verified" stickers — the sticker is a walking trust ad.
- **PR:** local press ("Mathura gets its own verified property platform") + data stories.

## 3. Dominating Mathura (the definition matters)
**Dominance = 60%+ of active dealers listing weekly · 70%+ of campus-corridor PG beds · #1–3 organic for every {type}×{locality} money query · 25+ qualified leads/day · dealers mention GharBazaar leads unprompted in the market.**
Tactics: response-rate culture (public badges make speed a status symbol) · demand-data gifts to dealers (weekly "what seekers wanted" WhatsApp digest — free, addictive, unreplicable by incumbents locally) · broker-association relationship (sponsor their Diwali meet; cheaper than one billboard) · every closed deal prompts a review + "sold via GharBazaar" story.

## 4. Delhi NCR — the disciplined beachhead
**Challenge accepted assumption:** general NCR buy/rent at launch is a capital bonfire (99acres/NoBroker home turf, CACs 10–20× Mathura, zero local supply advantage). **NCR launches as student housing ONLY:**
- Clusters: Greater Noida knowledge park (Sharda, Galgotias, Bennett, GBU, GNIOT), Noida sec-62/125 (Amity, JSS), North Campus DU + Mukherjee Nagar & Old Rajinder Nagar (UPSC coaching — massive, chronically underserved), South Campus, Jamia corridor.
- Same playbook: operator-first field onboarding per cluster, college pages, ambassadors, season timing. Each cluster is its own mini-Mathura with its own gate.
- General NCR real estate unlocks at Stage 4 (01-vision) — with funding, brand, and student-side demand data as the wedge ("we already own your children's housing search").

## 5. City expansion playbook (the repeatable unit)
60-day checklist per campus city: seed data (city/areas/universities rows — zero code) → hire 1–2 field ops locally → 150 listings incl. campus-corridor PGs → locality guides + college pages indexed → founding-dealer offer → season-timed ignition → gates reviewed monthly. **Expansion criterion: previous city contribution-positive AND playbook hit its numbers without founder physically present.** Cities queue (Stage 2–3): Agra, Aligarh (AMU!), Kanpur, Lucknow, Prayagraj, Bareilly, Gwalior, Jaipur.

## 6. Retention loops (the difference between traffic and a business)
- **Seeker:** saved-search WhatsApp/email alerts (THE loop) · price-drop pings · season reminders ("hostel hunt starts now") · post-close review ask keeps them in ecosystem for services.
- **Seller:** weekly performance digest ("214 views, 9 leads, quality score 71 — add photos for more") · demand-near-you · renewal nudges · founding/Trusted-Partner status to protect.
- **Referral:** dealer-refers-dealer (free month each) · student refer (₹200 cashback on referee's verified lease) · operator refers operator (featured week).

## 7. Network effects & moats (what compounds)
Listings→SEO→seekers→leads→dealers→listings (classic flywheel) · **review corpus** on PGs/dealers (unreplicable without our lead-verification) · **demand data** (search analytics nobody else has at this grain) · locality/college content library · verification reputation (one season of "GharBazaar listings are real" word-of-mouth in a town this size is permanent) · sold-price data (11§checklist) → future valuation moat.

## 8. Metrics cadence & kill criteria
Weekly founder dashboard: supply (net live listings, assisted %, freshness), demand (sessions, search→reveal funnel, CPL by channel), quality (approval SLA, fake-catch rate, response median), retention (alert CTR, D30 seller activity), revenue (post-switch: MRR, ARPU, churn).
**Honesty clause:** if Mathura hasn't hit liquidity gates by month 4 post-launch, we fix Mathura — we do not distract ourselves with a new city. Expansion is a reward, not an escape.

## 9. Team by stage
Launch: founder (sales/product) + 2 field ops + 1 support. Post-liquidity: +1 city lead (NCR student), +1 content/SEO. Stage 3: +city leads per city, +1 T&S/ops manager, first engineer hire beyond founder+AI tooling. Payroll stays behind contribution margin per city.

## 10. v1.1 — Founder Review additions

### The definition that everything inherits: "qualified lead"
> A **contact reveal or inquiry** with a valid phone, on a **live listing**, that receives a **seller response within 48h**, deduplicated per (seeker phone × listing × 7 days).
This is the unit of the north-star metric (01), the liquidity gates (05§0), dealer digests, and future lead-package pricing. One definition, computed one way, everywhere — metrics that drift across documents are how startups lie to themselves.

### Budget (directional)
Pre-launch one-offs ₹2–3L (legal/policies, brand, collateral, ambassador kit). Monthly run ₹1.7–2.1L (05§7) with marketing hard-capped at ₹50k until CPL proves out. Channel CAC targets: paid search CPL ≤ ₹60 (student) / ₹120 (buy); ambassador cost per verified student signup ≤ ₹80; field CAC per active dealer ≤ ₹800. Kill any channel 2× over target for 4 consecutive weeks.

### Risk scenarios & pre-committed responses
| Scenario | Response |
|---|---|
| Season slips (launch misses April window) | Do NOT rush-launch into dead months — build supply to 400+ listings, launch T-6 weeks before January intake; rentals/buy carry the interim |
| Broker association cold-shoulders us | Owner-first + operator-first supply push; dealers follow leads, not sentiment |
| Incumbent free-premium dump | 05§12 — accelerate value, never discount |
| Google update hits browse pages | Alerts/direct/WhatsApp retention carries demand; content + brand queries recover; no thin-page exposure by design (06) |
| Field hiring fails | Founder runs routes for 30 days — the playbook must survive being executed by its author |

### Channel additions from review
- **GBP-for-operators:** create/claim Google Business Profiles for PG operators linking to their GharBazaar listings — we take the local pack while incumbents ignore it, and we become the operator's entire web presence (switching cost = moat).
- **"Mathura Property" broadcast channel** (WhatsApp Channel): weekly new-listings + price notes digest — demand-side audience we own *before* they need an account; season-critical for students.
- **Referral integrity:** student cashback pays only after operator confirms lease (verified via lead status), one per phone identity; dealer referral credit after referee's 3rd live listing — gaming guards cross-ref 10 risk signals.
- **Editorial calendar:** content (06) tied to this doc's season map — admission (Mar–Jul), festival buying (Oct–Nov), year-end NRI visits (Dec–Jan).
